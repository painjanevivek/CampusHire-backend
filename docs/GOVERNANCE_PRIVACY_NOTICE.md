## What CampusHire processes

CampusHire processes account and institutional-membership data; student profile, education, skill, target-role, link, roadmap, and notification data; uploaded resumes and reviewed extractions; role, eligibility, application, and decision evidence; security/session metadata; and auditable administrator actions. Normal onboarding excludes age/date of birth, and protected or sensitive attributes must not enter semantic-match inputs.

## Why it is processed

Data supports institutional placement administration, deterministic eligibility, explainable role relevance, resume review, student readiness guidance, applications, notifications, security, recovery, and accountability. A readiness percentage is completion guidance—not a hiring probability. AI-assisted extraction and semantic relevance do not determine formal eligibility.

## Sharing and providers

Approved infrastructure may process database, cache, object, email, malware-scan, monitoring, and backup data. Gemini receives only the minimum delimited content for an enabled workflow. Exact providers, locations, subprocessors, transfer safeguards, and contracts remain institution-specific release gates. CampusHire does not sell student data.

## Student choices and rights

Students can correct current profile evidence in the product. There is no automated export endpoint or dedicated appeal endpoint; export, restriction, objection, historical-correction, and appeal requests must use the institution-approved contact process in `docs/DATA_RIGHTS_RETENTION.md` and `docs/APPEALS_MANUAL_REVIEW.md`. Eligible students can request deletion with the exact confirmation phrase. Existing applications create an explicit retention hold instead of a misleading deletion success.
