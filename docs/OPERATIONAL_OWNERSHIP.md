## Required operating decisions

Owners must approve SLO/alert thresholds, audit retention and authorized readers, support severity/response targets, after-hours coverage, vendor escalation, maintenance windows, deployment/rollback authority, privacy-right deadlines, and risk-acceptance expiry. A service may not be described as 24×7 until staffed coverage and tested contacts exist.
